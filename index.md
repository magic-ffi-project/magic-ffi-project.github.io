<!-- ABOUT SUMMARY -->
<section class="section">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="section__header">
      <span class="section__label">About the Project</span>
      <h2>Advancing Automotive Digital Forensics</h2>
      <p>The vehicle industry has a long tradition of working with safety, but cybersecurity forensics is a new and under-explored area. MAGIC bridges this gap.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card-grid card-grid--2">
      <div class="card card--accent">
        <div class="card__icon">&#x1F50D;</div>
        <div class="card__label">Challenge</div>
        <h3>Forensic Soundness in Vehicles</h3>
        <p>Upcoming regulations, including the EU&rsquo;s updated Product Liability Directive, place a greater burden of proof on vehicle manufacturers to demonstrate their cybersecurity features are free of defects.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card card--accent">
        <div class="card__icon">&#x1F6E1;</div>
        <div class="card__label">Solution</div>
        <h3>Automotive Digital Forensics (ADF)</h3>
        <p>MAGIC develops ADF solutions that guarantee forensic soundness, enhance trustworthiness of incident investigations, and identify cybersecurity-related vulnerabilities throughout the vehicle lifecycle.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card card--accent">
        <div class="card__icon">&#x1F916;</div>
        <div class="card__label">Innovation</div>
        <h3>AI-Powered Threat Detection</h3>
        <p>Leveraging modern AI technologies to automate the analysis of vehicle data, shift from reactive to proactive cybersecurity, and develop adaptive real-time Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS).</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card card--accent">
        <div class="card__icon">&#x1F4CB;</div>
        <div class="card__label">Compliance</div>
        <h3>Regulatory Alignment</h3>
        <p>Achieving compliance with UNECE R155 &amp; R156 and ISO/SAE 21434, while potentially proposing enhancements to these standards based on project findings.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div style="text-align:center; margin-top:36px;">
      <a href="/about/" class="btn btn--outline-dark">Read Full Project Description</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<!-- KEY OBJECTIVES -->
<section class="section section--alt">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="section__header">
      <span class="section__label">Objectives</span>
      <h2>What We Aim to Achieve</h2>
    </div>
    <ul class="objectives-list">
      <li>
        <div class="obj-num">G1</div>
        <div class="obj-text"><strong>Forensically Sound ADF Solutions</strong>Develop automotive digital forensics solutions that guarantee forensic soundness, enhance trustworthiness of incident investigations, and identify cybersecurity-related vulnerabilities.</div>
      </li>
      <li>
        <div class="obj-num">G2</div>
        <div class="obj-text"><strong>Real-Time ADF Mechanisms</strong>Implement real-time ADF mechanisms to enhance the adaptability of incident detection and protection against cyberattack threats.</div>
      </li>
      <li>
        <div class="obj-num">G3</div>
        <div class="obj-text"><strong>Adaptive Cyber-Resilience</strong>Develop adaptive cyber-resilience techniques that dynamically adjust to evolving threats and system states in autonomous driving systems.</div>
      </li>
      <li>
        <div class="obj-num">G4</div>
        <div class="obj-text"><strong>Regulatory Compliance</strong>Achieve compliance with, and potentially propose enhancements to, UNECE R155 &amp; R156 and ISO/SAE 21434.</div>
      </li>
      <li>
        <div class="obj-num">G5</div>
        <div class="obj-text"><strong>Knowledge Dissemination</strong>Disseminate knowledge and educate future experts, including mentoring at least two Ph.D. students and six master&rsquo;s students.</div>
      </li>
      <li>
        <div class="obj-num">G6</div>
        <div class="obj-text"><strong>Real-World Feasibility</strong>Evaluate the feasibility of proposed ADF solutions for real-world implementations through Proof of Value (PoV) assessments.</div>
      </li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</section>

<!-- WORK PACKAGES PREVIEW -->
<section class="section">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="section__header">
      <span class="section__label">Structure</span>
      <h2>Work Packages</h2>
      <p>MAGIC is organized into six work packages covering the full vehicle lifecycle from design to post-incident analysis.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card-grid card-grid--3">
      <div class="card">
        <div class="card__label">WP1</div>
        <h3>Project Management</h3>
        <p>Overall coordination, dissemination, and reporting activities across the full project duration.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <div class="card__label">WP2</div>
        <h3>Use Cases &amp; Literature</h3>
        <p>Identification of industrial use cases, state-of-the-art analysis, and gap identification in current ADF practices.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <div class="card__label">WP3</div>
        <h3>Pre-Operation</h3>
        <p>Reference ADF architecture for forensically enabled vehicles with secure logging and storage guidelines.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <div class="card__label">WP4</div>
        <h3>On-Operation</h3>
        <p>Forensic soundness, real-time threat detection, AI-based Intrusion Detection Systems, and data collection during vehicle operation.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <div class="card__label">WP5</div>
        <h3>Post-Operation</h3>
        <p>Management of digital evidence, IDS maintainability, adaptive cyber-resilience, and post-incident forensic analysis.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <div class="card__label">WP6</div>
        <h3>Dissemination &amp; PoV</h3>
        <p>Proof of Value demonstrations, workshops at international conferences, and education of future automotive cybersecurity experts.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
