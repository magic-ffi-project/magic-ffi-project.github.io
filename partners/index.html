Partners

The MAGIC consortium brings together leading Swedish industry and academic organisations with complementary expertise.

MAGIC is coordinated by Volvo Technology AB and brings together six partners from Swedish industry and academia, covering automotive OEMs, cybersecurity specialists, applied research, and engineering consultancy.

VT
Coordinator

Volvo Technology AB

Project coordinator and WP1 leader. Part of the Volvo Group, Volvo Technology AB provides deep industrial expertise in automotive systems engineering and cybersecurity. Coordinator: Prakash Jayaraman.

Sweden's leading technical university, providing academic leadership, research excellence in cybersecurity, dependable systems, and formal methods. Vice project leader for MAGIC.
